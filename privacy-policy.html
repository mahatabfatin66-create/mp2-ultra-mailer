Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Introduction

MP2 ULTRA MAILER is an email productivity application. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles information when users connect their Google account.

Google Account and Gmail Access

MP2 ULTRA MAILER uses Google's OAuth authorization system. Users choose their Google account and grant permission before the application can send email through Gmail.

Information We Access

With the user's permission, the application may access the information necessary to identify the authorized Google account and send email through Gmail.

How We Use Information

Gmail access is used only to provide the email-sending functionality requested by the user.

Data Storage

OAuth authorization information may be stored locally on the user's computer so that the user does not need to authorize the same account every time.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent users' personal information. We do not use Gmail data for advertising purposes.

Third-Party Services

The application uses Google APIs and Google OAuth to provide Gmail functionality. Google's own privacy policies and terms also apply to Google's services.

Revoking Access

Users can revoke the application's Google account access at any time through their Google Account security settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, please contact the application developer using the developer contact email provided on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
